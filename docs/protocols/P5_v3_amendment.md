## What the registration is, and what OSF allows

As read for v2 on 11 and 23 October 2026 (`docs/protocols/P5_v2_amendment.md`, D77, D82; paraphrases, nothing quoted): a registration cannot gain or lose files after submission, so a new version needs its own registration, made from a component holding exactly its files so that OSF archives them and nothing else, and the superseded registration is updated with a paragraph pointing to it, the same paragraph entered as the justification. v2 followed that path (D86); its stop was posted to 695FW as an update of the same kind on 25 October 2026 (schema response `6ade09278faa7545598a0050`; D90), and that notice says that a version 3 will be registered separately before any of its gates is run, and that 695FW will then be updated to point to it. The acts below do both.

What changed from version 2, with the decision-log entries (ROADMAP.md) that fixed each change; every other line of the protocol is byte-identical to version 2's frozen text, the title line apart:

- The reason (D89, D91). Version 2's exact reference failed gate G2 on its first attempt (9 of 18 pilot pairs were reference failures) and on its one retry at doubled lengths (5 of 18), the failures concentrated at the lowest noise level, where the posterior is sharpest; D89 reads them, as a reading labelled as such, as chains started uniformly on the parameter box with an isotropic first proposal that did not adapt. Version 3 makes the narrow change the plan of 25 October 2026 (`docs/protocols/P5_v3_plan.md`, D91) names, and nothing more.
- The sampler's start and first proposal (D91, D93, D94, D95). The exact reference's four chains now start at the solutions of a bounded least-squares optimiser run from 16 uniform starts, kept by a Laplace-mass rule (at most four), each perturbed by a capped Gaussian draw, with a first proposal scaled to the local Gauss–Newton covariance in place of 2.38²/4 · (1/12) · I; every constant is written into the protocol (The exact reference). Everything after the start, the adaptation, the lengths, the acceptance, the one repeat, the reweighting and the direct runs, and the storage, is version 2's. The change was developed and passed on the 36 pilot surfaces version 2's gate G2 had seen (D93, D94), and the sampler as written into the script was checked to reproduce that study's records, which it did exactly, by the transcription check of D95, run by the author. The record of each run carries its start and, under each prior, the covariance the ratio estimator's sampler reads.
- The ratio estimator's sampler (D91, Decision 3; D93, D94, D95). Its start and first proposal are the same, by a gradient route: the modes by L-BFGS-B with the exact gradient of the log-target from torch autograd on a float64 copy of the network, the covariance being the pair's from its reference record, a preconditioner only; the sampled distribution is unchanged.
- Gate G2's seeds (D95). Six fresh seeds for its pilot surfaces and its retry's, chosen before any run of version 3 by a rule fixed in advance, the six smallest primes above version 2's largest pilot seed, in the order the protocol names them: 151, 157 and 163; 167, 173 and 179. The clause that the retry's chains at the fixed points start where the first attempt's did is dropped, the start now depending on each surface; the go rule says that a run's core-seconds include its optimiser's. The tolerances, the one retry at doubled lengths and the budget are version 2's.
- The emulator (D88; D91, item 4). Gate G1 was run under version 2 and passed with no refinement; its build is adopted by its SHA-256 and not rebuilt.
- The records (D91, item 5; D95). Version 3's gate records go under new names (`output/p5v3_*`); version 2's stay in place as history.
- Declarations (D95). The Status line, the reason, what had been seen, the entries that read the scripts, the pilots, gate G1's outcome, the sittings and their models, and the checklist, whose boxes for prior knowledge, the reference sampler, the ratio estimator's sampler, the gate tolerances and the independent review were reopened and re-ticked on the author's word before freezing.

What is not changed: the question, the hypotheses and their thresholds; the priors, the cut-offs and the bins; the calibrators, their architecture and training; gates G0, G1 (its rule, its outcome adopted) and G3; gate G2's tolerances, its retry and its budget; the sealed test set (N = 400, seeds 20261002 and 20261022, never drawn); the scoring, the analysis and the prior-work section (the plan, "What v3 does not change"; D91).

Why: so that the reference whose failure stopped version 2 is replaced by one shown to work on the surfaces that broke it, with the change confined to the sampler's start and first proposal and declared, the rest of the design standing as registered, and so that version 3's gates run on surfaces not yet seen (D91).
