Pre-registration, version 2, of study P5 of the RoughVolLab research programme (Michael Lumor; https://github.com/Michaellumor/roughvollab). It supersedes version 1, registered on 2 October 2026 as OSF APSF7 (https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/APSF7), which remains the record of what was registered then. The authoritative document is the attached file, `P5_protocol_v2_draft.md`, byte-identical to the file at commit `<freeze-commit>` of the repository (SHA-256 `<sha256>`), frozen on `<freeze-date>`. Beside it are attached the files it binds where it is silent, the six P5 scripts, the five forward-model modules they import (`rough_heston_cf.py`, `layer4_calibrate_surface.py`, `layer4_calibrate.py`, `rough_heston.py`, `roughvol_core.py`), both requirements files and the decision log, `ROADMAP.md`, each byte-identical to its file at that commit, their hashes listed in the protocol's Status line.

What changed from version 1, with the decision-log entries (ROADMAP.md) that fixed each change:

- The question (D55). Version 1's four predictions followed from Bayes' rule and the parameter box, so an exact posterior would have confirmed them as readily as a network. Version 2 asks whether a learned calibrator is more prior-driven than the exact posterior under its own training prior, and measures each calibrator against that posterior.
- The model (D55, D64). The forward model is a frozen Chebyshev emulator of the rough Heston implied-volatility surface, shared by the training data and the exact reference, in place of the pricer called directly; its accuracy is gated (G1) against the noise level.
- The observable, the grid, the box and the resolution (D58, D59, D61, D62). Each quote is the model implied volatility at the parameter point's own standardised moneyness, closing a leak version 1 inherited from the repository's D38 set-up (D58); the grid is D38's five maturities by seven standardised-moneyness points; the box is H [0.02, 0.48], the priors' support in both versions and by rule not narrowed by the pilot, with ν [0.05, 0.35], ρ [−0.90, −0.70] and ξ₀ [0.04, 0.25] narrowed from version 1's ranges by a pilot of the pricer (gate G0) run before freezing under a criterion written down before the run (D59, D61, D62); the pricer's resolution is fixed by the same pilot.
- Noise (D65). Three levels, 0.1, 0.3 and 0.5 volatility points, as a design factor, in place of one level of 0.1.
- The priors (D69). The two Beta priors on H, with means 0.12 and 0.32 on the same support as in version 1, now have their parameters written in, with equal variance.
- The exact reference and gate G2 (D66). The exact posterior of each surface is computed by adaptive Markov-chain Monte Carlo with stated acceptance criteria and checked against a grid posterior; version 1 took the Fisher information and the Cramér–Rao bound as the ground truth, which version 2 does not compute (D81).
- The calibrators and gate G3 (D67, D68). A five-member deep ensemble and a neural ratio estimator, with architecture, optimiser, stopping rule, seeds and failure rules written in; five training seeds per calibrator, prior and noise level; G3 tests the recovery of ξ₀ and the stability of the error in H under a doubling of the training set, and no longer reads "trained on prior A" or "in every quintile".
- Stratification (D70). Fixed cut-offs on the exact posterior's contraction of H under prior A, at 0.9 and 0.5, with a floor on bin occupancy, in place of quintiles of the Cramér–Rao bound computed on the test set.
- The hypotheses (D71). Four hypotheses with new measures and thresholds, scored per calibrator and pooled over noise levels; H-c expects a leak of prior dependence into the identified regime, the reverse of version 1's expectation there; the null requires H-a confirmed, the other three refuted and the interval-fidelity measure within a stated band (D81).
- The analysis (D70, D71, D75). A crossed bootstrap over training seeds and test pairs in place of a bootstrap over test surfaces (D71); a re-scoring at the cut-offs shifted by ±0.05 that decides nothing (D70); a descriptive dose-response (D71); the analysis script committed before any result exists (D75). The whole-interval verdict rule is kept from version 1.
- Dropped from version 1 without a counterpart. The control at home (version 1's coverage on test H drawn from the training prior); the optional tenfold-cleaner noise level, superseded by the three levels; and version 1's stated reason for the 0.20 gap between the priors' means, the draft now giving the gap in standard deviations of the priors. Recorded in the decision sheet's "Differences from v1".
- Prior work (D76, D84). The section re-reads version 1's papers at source and records where their text does not bear out version 1's sentences, and covers prior-sensitivity analysis and the literature on unfaithful amortised posteriors; the novelty claim is limited to the identifiability map and the measurement against an exact posterior in rough-volatility calibration.
- Code before freezing (D59 to D62, D72 to D75). Version 1 was registered before any P5 code existed. Version 2 is frozen with the pilot of the pricer run and its output committed, and with every script committed; no emulator of the design, reference posterior or trained network exists at freezing, the test suites having built only toy objects: two small interpolants from the pricer, neither the design's emulator, posteriors on toy linear maps and small networks on a toy linear map (D81, D82).
- Pre-freeze fixes (D81), after an independent review on 22 October 2026 (a referee's read, a code-against-protocol audit and that sitting's own checks, all read-only; its record is in the repository): the null corrected so that a faithful calibrator can meet it (H-a confirmed, the other three refuted); a per-pair coverage covariate and a secondary analysis on the pairs both priors cover, with the full-set verdicts' extrapolation outside the training support stated as a limitation; the reweighting floor set on the weighted chain's effective size; G2's tolerance extended to the posterior mean under the second prior and the 5% and 95% quantiles under the first, each tolerance at least four Monte Carlo standard errors of the sampled figure, with two fixed pilot points near the ends of H, one retry with doubled sampler lengths and a go or no-go on cost; the test set fixed at 400 parameter points with its noise seed; one re-pricing of a point with a quote that is not finite at G1, and a per-cell pass or fail at G3 at the largest training size within budget; Stage 1 declared from the references before any network scores the test set; the scripts at the freeze commit binding wherever the text is silent, with their hashes and the numpy and scipy versions listed; and the runs not declared before declared.
